THE ESSENTIALS

No personal-details form is hosted here. Your selection opens an external referral service. Meta PageView measurement starts automatically when this page loads, subject to browser privacy signals and tracking restrictions. Separate ClickFlare referral tracking is active on the landing page.

01

Your privacy, at a glance

This policy explains how this auto insurance referral website handles information when you browse or choose an insurance option. It covers this website only. A quote provider or other website you visit has its own privacy policy.

You can browse this page without entering your name, email address, phone number, payment details, or policy number. There is currently no form on this website that collects those details.

02

Information involved in your visit

  • Your selection. Choosing YES or NO indicates whether you are currently insured. The page uses that selection for its button interaction. The answer is not included in the Meta event sent by this website.
  • Email correspondence. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this to respond and handle your request through our email provider.
  • Technical information. Hosting and network providers may receive your IP address, browser information, requested pages, and request times when delivering this website. Their handling of server logs depends on the hosting service.
  • Referral information. Following a quote link sends your browser to an external tracking or referral destination. That service receives connection information and may receive a referring page address, subject to your browser’s settings.
03

Advertising measurement & cookies

Meta Pixel loads automatically and sends a PageView event for the page visit; this site does not show a consent popup. Clicking a quote button does not send a Lead event or any other Meta event. We do not include your insurance-status answer or contact details in these events, and automatic event configuration is disabled in our integration. Meta may also receive browser, device, cookie, and connection information through its script and use this information for measurement and advertising under its own policies.

Meta may set advertising cookies such as _fbp and _fbc. This website does not use a consent-preference panel or store new consent choices. Existing browser privacy signals (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track) prevent this integration from loading. Events are not stored in our own application database.

Measurement is disabled when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. It is also disabled for page or referrer URLs containing unsupported parameters or fragments, to reduce unintended information sharing. See Meta’s privacy policy and cookie policy for its processing practices.

04

Your choices

You can leave the website without selecting an option. Your browser can block or remove cookies, limit third-party tracking, and control the information shared when you follow links. These controls may affect features on other websites.

You can review advertising preferences in your Meta account, use your browser’s cookie and tracking controls, or enable Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. These browser signals disable this site’s Meta integration. They do not disable the separate ClickFlare referral tag. Browser controls do not erase information already received by providers.

05

External providers & sharing

The landing page loads a ClickFlare tag from globalvisitlog.com on page load. The tag request includes the referring URL, current page URL, page title, timestamp, and a campaign identifier when available from the URL or cf_cpid cookie. The service also receives normal connection information such as your IP address and browser details. The loaded service may use cookies and additional tracking according to its configuration. Quote buttons navigate to https://globalvisitlog.com/cf/click.

ClickFlare runs separately from Meta measurement. Turning off Meta measurement does not disable ClickFlare. Avoid including personal or sensitive information in page URLs.

This website is a referral page, not the insurer that may ultimately provide a quote. The external referral destination, hosting provider, and any active measurement provider may process information needed for their services. Information you submit after leaving this website is handled by the receiving service.

Review the receiving website’s privacy policy, contact details, and consent language before entering personal information. Clicking a button on this page does not itself submit an insurance application.

06

Retention & security

The page does not have an application database for your insurance selection. Its local measurement queue lasts only for the current page session. Support correspondence is retained as needed to address your request and applicable recordkeeping requirements. Hosting logs, external referral records, and advertising records follow the retention practices of the relevant providers. Providers may process information in countries other than your own.

No website or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Avoid placing sensitive information in page URLs or sending it through channels that are not intended to collect it.

07

Privacy requests & younger visitors

Depending on applicable law and the information involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain uses of your personal information. For information submitted on a provider’s website, use that provider’s published privacy-request channel.

This website is intended for adults seeking insurance information. It is not designed to collect information from children.

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@saveautoinfo.com. Describe your request without sending policy numbers, payment information, or other sensitive documents. We may need enough information to verify and respond to your request. Available rights and response obligations depend on applicable law.

08

Policy updates

This policy should be updated when the website’s collection, tracking, providers, or contact arrangements change. The date at the top identifies this version. Please review it before providing information or following a quote link.
